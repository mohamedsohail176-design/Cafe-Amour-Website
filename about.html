Our story

More than a coffee, it's Amour.

Every cafe serves coffee. We wanted to build a room worth staying in.

Cafe AMOUR started with a simple idea — a neighbourhood spot in Ramapuram where the coffee is taken seriously, but nothing else is taken too seriously.

We built the menu around what we'd actually want to order: filter coffee and Madras-inspired signatures alongside matcha, thickshakes, wood-fired pizza and Italian pasta — a menu that doesn't try to be one thing, because our regulars don't want just one thing either.

The space is designed to work as hard as the menu does. Warm wood, low lighting, cane and leather seating — built for a quiet morning coffee, a long lunch, or a table pushed together for a celebration.

What makes us different: we're a cafe, a full dining kitchen, and an events space, all in one room — so the same place you grab a cortado on a Tuesday is the place you can book for a birthday on a Saturday.
